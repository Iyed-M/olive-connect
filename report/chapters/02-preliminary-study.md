# Preliminary study

The preliminary study identifies the cooperative's coordination problem: data
about orchards, requests, teams, equipment, mills and alerts is otherwise
spread across paper, spreadsheets and phone calls. That fragmentation causes
late planning, double declarations, poor visibility during collection, and
weak traceability at the mill.

OliveConnect addresses the problem around five coherent features:

1. collection and orchard management;
2. field alerts and notifications;
3. resource management;
4. a public and private geospatial map;
5. dashboard KPIs and operational reporting.

The actors are Visitor, Farmer, Collection manager, Team leader, Logistics
manager, Mill manager and Admin. The [preliminary study](../../docs/01-preliminary-study.md)
explains their responsibilities and the vocabulary used throughout this report.

The scope is intentionally vertical. Each milestone crosses the browser,
REST API, business service, persistence layer and deployment. This lets the
team demonstrate a complete path early and makes ownership, error handling and
operability visible rather than postponing them to a final integration phase.
